Approach

I work where psychology, business and computer science meet — turning complex systems, processes and data into decisions people can understand, trust and act upon. Day to day that is Head of Automation & AI: helping set the strategy for agentic AI and intelligent automation, then building it — across manufacturing, supply chain, finance and sales order fulfilment, on a frame of detect, explain, recommend, execute.

It began with kaizen: watching how work is actually done. Then payments rails, on the product side of an institution settling upwards of five trillion US dollars a day in global FX, where the largest investment banks in the world care less about elegance than about whether the rails hold. Then co-founding a retail bank's robotic process automation practice and scaling it bank-wide; then digital platforms, machine learning, intelligent automation and UX across finance and manufacturing; now agents.

The unit of work keeps changing — a procedure, a bot, a model, an agent. The question never does: can someone trust the answer enough to act on it? That is what everything on this list is really testing.
